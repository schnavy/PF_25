The 5-channel video installation inside the body of the ship MS Dauerwelle as part of the “Breminale 2026” is the result
of a collective site-specific exploration, referencing methodologies and approaches of the Situationist International
movement.The core of the work is a collective chain of associations, ranging along a few predefined keywords.

*In girum imus nocte ecce et consumimur igni.* This ancient palindrome and title of a 1978 film by Guy Debord represents
the bi-directional nature of the chain of associations and contains the basis for the work’s title in its translation:
“We go around in circles in the night and are consumed by fire”
*Tide level* – The River Weser in Bremen is subject to the tide levels of the North Sea, causing the ships on it to move
vertically, even when anchored to a fixed place.
*1962*, as the birth year of this ship Dauerwelle, *Water* and *Drift* (connecting to the Situationist technique of
Dérive as well as the mode of movement of a ship)

<br/>

...→ In girum imus nocte ecce et consumimur igni → Fire → Circle → Spiral → Google Maps → Direction → Tide level → Tide → Tide gauge → Amsterdam → North Sea → North Sea flood of 1962 → 1962 → Fluxus → Düsseldorf → Rhine → North Sea → Water → Earth → Human Geography → Cutural Geography → Psychogeography(Psychological) → Dérive (Drift) → Drift → Drift Ice → Navigation → Canal → France → Avant-garde → Letterist International → In girum imus...

<br/>

Navigating along and between these keywords, using hyperlinks in the online encyclopedia “Wikipedia”, a chain of 29
words emerged. For each word a short video clip was produced by a member of the group, which then bacame a video
installation inside the main body of the ship. To resemble the connections and to move the bodies of the spectators
through the ship, the clips where orchestrated algorithmicly to “jump” through 5 wooden projection screens of varying
dimensions. Once a clip ended, on of projections surfaces in sight lights up, displaying the following clip. Using a
weighted randomness with restrictions and rules, a certrain flow of bodies throughout the whole ship created a large
number of possible paths though the ships interiour.
