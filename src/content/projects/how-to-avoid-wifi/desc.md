The campus of the "École de Beaux Arts Marseille" is situated beautifully in the "Parc National des Calanques"
outside the city of Marseille. It is a big area, with a lot of wildlife activity, while only the small central building
has Wi-Fi coverage. This almost two hour hike is the result of trying to reach every corner of the campus, being guided
by the absence of Wi-Fi connection. I let my phone guide my route by checking
for the connection, and as soon as the Network popped up, I changed the direction.

<br/>

It was printed as part of a small publication by the international students "Comment survivre à son échange Erasmus".
