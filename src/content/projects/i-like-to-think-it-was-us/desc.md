The story of a rebelious JPEG finding it‘s way through the materiality of infrastructure towards the AWS datacenter
„us-east-1“ on the day of it‘s 14 hour failure (20.10.2025)
The video is composed of extremely upscaled found footage, as well as self-shot phone footage, breaking the conveyed
image to be perceived as the materiality that it composes. A sense of scale that moves together with the narrator
through air, sea, earth, cables, nodes and data facilities, slowly leading to a system collapse.
It started out as a selection of performance scores making the audience become the JPEG. It further evolved to a video
work, which is still in process.
